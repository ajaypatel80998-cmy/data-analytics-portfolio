Why deliveries slipped in the monsoon

PostgreSQL · Python (pandas, scikit-learn) · Power BI

Ops believed rain was the cause. Was it?

Joined four years of shipment records with hourly weather and depot staffing data — about 2.1 million rows. Rainfall explained far less variance than expected once I controlled for depot load. The real driver was that the same two sorting hubs absorbed overflow from every delayed route.

Rerouting overflow to three hubs cut median late-delivery time by 41 minutes.

The customers who leave quietly

SQL · Python · Tableau

Which subscribers churn without ever complaining?

Built monthly cohorts for 180,000 subscribers and modelled churn with logistic regression. Customers who filed a support ticket were less likely to leave. Silence was the warning sign: a 30-day drop in login frequency predicted cancellation better than any complaint.

A weekly "quiet accounts" list recovered 1,240 subscribers in six months.

Six-month retention by engagement cohort.

What a discount is really worth

BigQuery · dbt · Excel

Do promo codes create sales or just move them earlier?

Compared purchase timing for 60,000 shoppers around four promo windows. Around 44% of promo revenue came from customers who had already added the item to a cart that week — demand pulled forward, not created. Rebuilt the margin dashboard to report incremental revenue rather than gross promo sales.

Two of five recurring campaigns were retired; contribution margin rose 3.2 points.

Returns flatten sharply past a 20% discount.
